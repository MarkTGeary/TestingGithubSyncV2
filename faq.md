# Frequently Asked Questions

The quick answers to the things people ask us most. Can't find what you're looking for below? Our support team is only a message away and always happy to help.

### 🔑 How do I reset my password?

Go to **Settings → Security** and select **Change Password**. For your safety, you'll be signed out of other devices once the change is complete. If you've forgotten your current password entirely, use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in screen.

### 🗂️ Can I have multiple workspaces?

Absolutely. You can create and switch between as many workspaces as you like from the **workspace menu** in the top-left corner. Each workspace keeps its own members, content, and settings completely separate.

### 💬 How do I contact support?

Use the **in-app messenger** for the fastest response, or email our support team directly. We typically reply within a few hours during business days, and urgent issues are prioritized.

### 💾 Is my data backed up?

Yes. We run **automated daily backups** stored across multiple secure, geographically separate locations, so your data stays protected even in the unlikely event of a regional outage.
