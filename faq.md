# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I reset my password?** Go to Security in your account settings and
select Change Password.

**Can I have multiple workspaces?** Yes. You can create and switch between
workspaces from the workspace menu.

**How do I contact support?** Use the in-app messenger or email our support
team directly.

**Is my data backed up?** Yes, we run automated daily backups stored across
multiple locations.
